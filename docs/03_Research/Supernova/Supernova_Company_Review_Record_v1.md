# Purpose

This document defines the minimum review record used to apply Supernova governance to an Approved Company.

It converts the Replacement Risk definition into an operational review contract without turning qualitative governance into an automatic score or trade rule.

---

# Review Record

Each material company review should preserve:

```text
Company
Review Date
Evidence As Of
Company Score
Portfolio State
Leadership Role
Replacement Risk
Primary Risk Driver
Evidence
Assessment
Recommended Action
Governance Decision
Approver
```

The four Replacement Risk axes must be explicitly considered:

```text
Leadership Threat
Moat Deterioration
Growth / Execution Deterioration
Thesis Integrity
```

The axes are not independently scored in v1. The reviewer records the relevant evidence and assessment, then assigns one overall qualitative Replacement Risk level.

---

# Evidence As Of

`Review Date` records when the governance review was performed. `Evidence As Of` records the latest date through which material evidence was considered. These fields must remain separate because company disclosures may be published before or after the review session.

If material evidence is stale, the record should identify the limitation rather than imply current knowledge.

---

# Review Record Template

For each company, use the following minimum structure:

```text
Company
Review Date
Evidence As Of
Company Score
  Theme Exposure
  Competitive Moat
  Leadership Position
  Growth Quality
  Execution Quality
Portfolio State
Leadership Role
Replacement Risk
  Leadership Threat
  Moat Deterioration
  Growth / Execution Deterioration
  Thesis Integrity
Primary Risk Driver
Evidence Summary
Assessment
Recommended Action
Governance Decision
Approver
```

The five Company Score dimensions are scored separately. The four Replacement Risk axes remain qualitative assessment lenses and are not mechanically weighted.

---

# Evidence Rules

1. Material claims should retain source provenance where practical.
2. Primary company disclosures should be preferred for company operating evidence.
3. Reliable secondary sources may supplement primary evidence, particularly for competitive developments.
4. Market price movement alone is insufficient evidence for Replacement Risk.
5. A challenger may be recorded when relevant, but the absence of a challenger does not reduce the obligation to assess the company's own deterioration.
6. Historical reviews should remain traceable so that changed evidence can be distinguished from changed judgment.

---

# Governance Interpretation

The review record does not itself change Portfolio State or Leadership Role.

The sequence remains:

```text
Evidence
    ↓
Assessment
    ↓
Company Score / Replacement Risk
    ↓
Governance Decision
    ↓
Portfolio Transition (if approved)
```

Replacement Risk escalation is a review trigger, not an automatic trading action.

# Governance Reproducibility Review — 2026-10-05

The five-company baseline was used to test whether a reviewer can reconstruct the governance record from the documented inputs.

## Result

**Governance-level reproducibility: PASS**

**Independent numeric score reproducibility: NOT REQUIRED IN V1**

The framework provides deterministic Company Score aggregation once the five dimension scores are assigned. The dimension scores themselves remain evidence-assisted judgments supported by qualitative anchors. Therefore two qualified reviewers may reasonably assign different dimension scores while still following the same approved methodology. V1 requires traceability and defensibility, not identical human judgment.

## Required Reconstruction Path

A complete review must allow the following reconstruction without undocumented intermediate decisions:

```text
Evidence + Evidence As Of
        ↓
Dimension Assessments
        ↓
Five Dimension Scores
        ↓
Weighted Company Score
        ↓
Leadership Role / Portfolio State assessment
        ↓
Four Replacement Risk axes
        ↓
Overall Replacement Risk
        ↓
Primary Risk Driver
        ↓
Recommended Action
        ↓
Governance Decision
```

## Findings

1. **Company Score is reproducible from the recorded dimension scores.** The five-company baseline was recalculated against D-045 and the published totals reconcile after rounding.
2. **Dimension-score judgment is intentionally not mechanically reproducible.** The current qualitative anchors are sufficient for governance traceability but do not prescribe exact numeric cut-points.
3. **Replacement Risk is reproducible at the governance-record level.** The four axes, overall level, evidence, and assessment are explicitly recorded without requiring a challenger.
4. **Governance actions must use the canonical D-046 action vocabulary.** `Continue` is the canonical action; descriptive phrases such as “Continue Accumulation” should not be stored as a separate action type.
5. **Primary Risk Driver must describe the dominant concern, not merely repeat an assessment axis.** Where no material concern exists, `None material` is valid.
6. **Evidence As Of is a temporal cutoff, not a publication date.** It identifies the latest evidence considered by the review.

## Residual V1 Limitation

V1 does not define a numeric calibration table for converting qualitative evidence into each 0–100 dimension score. This is deliberate. Introducing fixed numeric thresholds would create false precision and could unintentionally turn Company Score into an automatic governance rule. If later reviews show unacceptable reviewer divergence, calibration can be added through a future governance decision rather than inferred operationally.

## Conclusion

The Supernova governance framework is sufficiently specified for recurring human-led review. No additional Replacement Risk scoring formula is required for V1. Future work should focus on applying the same review contract consistently and preserving evidence provenance rather than adding more scoring machinery.
