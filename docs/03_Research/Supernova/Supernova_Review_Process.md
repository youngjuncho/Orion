# Purpose

This document defines the operational review process used by Supernova.

---

# Review Levels

Monthly Review

Quarterly Review

Annual Review

---

# Monthly Review

Objective:

Monitor approved companies.

Review Items:

* Leadership status
* Competitive position
* Replacement risk
* Theme alignment

Possible Outcomes:

* Continue Accumulation
* Watch
* Review Required

---

# Quarterly Review

Objective:

Evaluate candidate universe and themes.

Review Items:

* Candidate additions
* Candidate removals
* Challenger emergence
* Theme health

Possible Outcomes:

* Universe update
* Theme update
* No Action

---

# Annual Review

Objective:

Evaluate framework effectiveness.

Review Items:

* Theme framework
* Scoring methodology
* Candidate universe quality
* Portfolio construction methodology

Possible Outcomes:

* Framework update
* No Action

# Review Triggers

Leader deterioration

Theme deterioration

Replacement risk escalation

Emerging challenger

## Replacement Risk Review Rule

Replacement Risk is assessed independently from Company Score. The review should
consider Leadership Threat, Moat Deterioration, Growth / Execution Deterioration,
and Thesis Integrity. A challenger is not required for risk escalation.

Replacement Risk escalation triggers further governance review; it does not
automatically create a Replace or Retire action.

## Governance Reproducibility Rule

A material review is considered complete only when another reviewer can reconstruct
the reasoning path from Evidence and Evidence As Of through the five dimension
scores, Company Score, Replacement Risk assessment, Recommended Action, and
Governance Decision. V1 does not require identical independent numeric judgment
for qualitative dimension scores.

The canonical action vocabulary is `Continue`, `Promote`, `Review`, `Replace`,
and `Retire`. Descriptive wording may appear in rationale but does not create a
new action type.

# Review Outputs

Leadership Role

Portfolio State

Replacement Risk

Theme Health

Evidence / Assessment

Recommended Actions

Governance Decision

Each material company decision should preserve:

* Company Score
* Portfolio State
* Leadership Role
* Replacement Risk
* Action
* Rationale
* Evidence Summary
* Review Date
* Approver

The score is an input to this decision, not an automatic state-transition rule.

---

# Relationship With Portfolio

Price changes alone do not trigger review outcomes.

Leadership, structural theme changes, competitive deterioration, and thesis invalidation are primary review drivers.

A review finding is not automatically a trade. Research / Detection, Governance Decision, and Portfolio Transition remain separate stages.

Monthly DCA continues for Approved Companies unless a governance decision changes the Approved Company list.
